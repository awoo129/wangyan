# Highlights

## Key Points

**1. First evidence of age-dependent CIITA discordance in sepsis immunoparalysis**  
*Pediatric CIITA↓ vs Adult CIITA↑ reveals fundamental age-related immunological differences*

**2. Validated three-gene pediatric signature (CIITA, HLA-DRA, HLA-DPB1)**  
*AUC=0.870/0.821 in discovery/validation cohorts provides robust diagnostic tool*

**3. Distinct mechanistic patterns: "Pure Suppression" vs "Compensatory Inflammation"**  
*Children show coordinated IFN-γ-STAT1-IRF1 axis collapse; adults show paradoxical CIITA rise*

**4. Challenges universal application of adult-derived immunoparalysis biomarkers**  
*Age-specific biomarkers needed for pediatric sepsis risk stratification*

**5. Informs targeted immunomodulatory therapy across the lifespan**  
*Findings guide distinct treatment strategies for pediatric vs adult patients*
