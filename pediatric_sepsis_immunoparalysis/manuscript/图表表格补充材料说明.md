## Figure Legend Summary

### Figure 1: Study Design and Analytical Pipeline
**Description**: Overview of the four-route analytical framework examining age-dependent CIITA expression in sepsis-induced immunoparalysis across six independent cohorts (n=957 total).

**Key Elements**:
- Pediatric cohorts: GSE26440 (discovery), GSE26378 (validation), GSE13904 (external)
- Adult cohorts: GSE65682 (discovery/validation), GAinS (external)
- Route A: Pediatric IPS model construction
- Route B: Adult IPS model construction
- Route C: Pediatric external validation
- Route D: Mechanism analysis

### Figure 2: CIITA Expression Comparison
**Description**: Age-dependent discordance of CIITA expression showing opposite directional changes in pediatric (downregulation) vs adult (upregulation) immunoparalysis.

**Key Data**:
- Pediatric: log2FC = -0.576, p = 7.97×10⁻⁹
- Adult: log2FC = +0.122, p = 0.0034

### Figure 3: ROC Curves
**Description**: Diagnostic performance of IPS models showing robust discrimination for immunoparalysis classification.

**Key Metrics**:
- Pediatric: AUC = 0.870 (training), 0.821 (validation)
- Adult: AUC = 0.830 (training), 0.696 (validation)

### Figure 4: Mechanistic Hypothesis
**Description**: Proposed framework explaining age-dependent mechanisms: "Pure Suppression" (pediatric) vs "Compensatory Inflammation" (adult).

**Key Insight**: IFN-γ-STAT1-IRF1 axis shows coordinated downregulation in children but compensatory upregulation in adults.

### Figure 5: MHC II Heatmap
**Description**: Coordinated downregulation of CIITA and MHC II genes in pediatric immunoparalysis, confirming CIITA as master regulator.
