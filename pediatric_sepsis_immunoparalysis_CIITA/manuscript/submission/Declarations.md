## 1. Ethics approval and consent to participate

**Statement:**

This study analyzed publicly available datasets from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) and ArrayExpress repositories. The original studies from which these datasets were derived received appropriate ethical approvals and informed consent from participants or their legal guardians. The ethical statements from the original studies are available in the following publications:

- GSE26440 and GSE26378: Wong HR et al., PLoS Medicine 2019 [doi:10.1371/journal.pmed.1002899]
- GSE13904: Wong HR et al., Journal of Pediatrics 2019 [doi:10.1016/j.jpeds.2018.09.064]
- GSE65682 (MARS Consortium): Davenport EE et al., Lancet Respiratory Medicine 2016 [doi:10.1016/S2213-2600(16)00046-1]
- E-MTAB-4451 (GAinS Consortium): Burnham KL et al., American Journal of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine 2017 [doi:10.1164/rccm.201608-1685OC]

No new human subjects were recruited for this retrospective bioinformatic analysis. All data were de-identified prior to public deposition.
